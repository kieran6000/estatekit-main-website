## What does the ad actually offer a homeowner?

Most seller ads offer something a homeowner wants before they're ready to list, such as a free valuation or a look at recent sales on their street. The offer has to be worth swapping a name and number for.

## Who sees the ads?

Housing ads on Meta run under a special ad category, so targeting by age, gender or a tight postcode isn't allowed. The ad reaches a broad area and the questions on the form do the sorting.

## What happens once someone fills in the form?

Qualified leads go straight to the agent's WhatsApp, and the agent makes the call. Leads who aren't ready yet get followed up automatically until they are. You can see what this looks like for real agents on our [results page](/results).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why ask questions on the form?

They separate homeowners who are ready to talk from the ones who are just curious, so your calls go to the right people first.

### How quickly should I call a new lead?

As soon as you can. A lead who hears from you within minutes still remembers filling in the form.
